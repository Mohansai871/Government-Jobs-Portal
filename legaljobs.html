Legal Jobs in the Government Sector

Legal Advisor

Department: Department of Justice

Responsibilities:

  • Provide legal advice to government officials
  • Draft and review legal documents
  • Represent the department in legal proceedings

Eligibility:

  • J.D. from an accredited law school
  • Admission to the bar
  • 5+ years of legal experience
Assistant District Attorney

Department: District Attorney's Office

Responsibilities:

  • Prosecute criminal cases
  • Prepare and present legal arguments
  • Conduct legal research and case preparation

Eligibility:

  • J.D. from an accredited law school
  • Admission to the bar
  • 3+ years of prosecutorial experience
Government Counsel

Department: City Attorney's Office

Responsibilities:

  • Provide legal counsel to city officials
  • Handle litigation and legal disputes
  • Draft legal opinions and policy recommendations

Eligibility:

  • J.D. from an accredited law school
  • Admission to the bar
  • 7+ years of legal experience in government
