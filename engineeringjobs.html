Engineering Jobs in the Government Sector

Civil Engineer

Departments: Public Works Department (PWD), Railways, National Highways Authority of India (NHAI)

Key Roles: Construction and maintenance of roads, bridges, public buildings, and other infrastructure.

Eligibility Details
Qualification B.E./B.Tech in Civil Engineering
Age Limit 21-30 years (varies by department)
Exams UPSC Engineering Services (ESE), State PSC Exams

Mechanical Engineer

Departments: Railways, Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC)

Key Roles: Operation and maintenance of machinery in power plants, railways, and manufacturing industries.

Eligibility Details
Qualification B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering
Age Limit 21-30 years
Exams UPSC ESE, GATE, PSU Exams

Electrical Engineer

Departments: Power Grid Corporation, Electricity Boards, Indian Railways, NTPC

Key Roles: Power generation, maintenance of electrical grids, railway electrification.

Eligibility Details
Qualification B.E./B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
Age Limit 21-30 years
Exams UPSC ESE, GATE, State Electricity Board Exams

Computer Engineer

Departments: National Informatics Centre (NIC), Ministry of Electronics, Indian Railways, BSNL

Key Roles: IT systems development, cybersecurity, e-governance, database management.

Eligibility Details
Qualification B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science/IT
Age Limit 21-30 years
Exams UPSC ESE, GATE, PSU Exams

Chemical Engineer

Departments: Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC), Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL), HPCL

Key Roles: Management of chemical processes in refineries, gas plants, and fertilizer units.

Eligibility Details
Qualification B.E./B.Tech in Chemical Engineering
Age Limit 21-30 years
Exams GATE, PSU Exams
