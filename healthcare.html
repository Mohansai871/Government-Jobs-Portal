Healthcare Jobs

Registered Nurse (RN)

Job Description: Provide direct patient care, administer medications, and coordinate with healthcare teams.

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in Nursing (BSN), valid RN license, and at least 2 years of clinical experience.

Medical Laboratory Technician

Job Description: Perform laboratory tests, analyze results, and ensure the accuracy of data.

Eligibility: Associate's degree in Medical Laboratory Technology or related field, certification preferred.

Pharmacist

Job Description: Dispense medications, provide patient consultations, and manage pharmaceutical inventories.

Eligibility: Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D) degree, valid state license, and experience in a clinical setting.

Physical Therapist

Job Description: Develop treatment plans for patients, perform therapeutic exercises, and assess patient progress.

Eligibility: Doctorate in Physical Therapy (DPT), state licensure, and strong interpersonal skills.

Healthcare Administrator

Job Description: Oversee healthcare facilities, manage staff, and ensure compliance with regulations.

Eligibility: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Healthcare Administration or related field, and managerial experience.
